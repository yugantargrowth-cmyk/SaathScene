Terms & Code of Conduct

Clear Rules & Boundaries • Ahmedabad, Gujarat

Essential Principle: SaathScene operates strictly as a non-sexual, social companionship service. We provide friendly social company for public outings. We are not a dating platform, matrimonial broker, or escort service.

1. Age Requirement (Strict 18+)

You must be at least 18 years of age to submit an enquiry or apply as a companion. Submissions from minors are strictly prohibited and immediately deleted.

2. Nature of the Service & Non-Sexual Scope

  • All outings are strictly limited to platonic, social companionship in public spaces.
  • No romantic, intimate, or sexual conduct, advances, solicitation, or harassment is permitted under any circumstances.
  • Any violation results in immediate outing cancellation and permanent reporting/banning.

3. Venue Boundaries: Strictly Public Locations

  • All meetings must begin, take place, and conclude in verified, busy public venues (e.g. registered Garba grounds, recognized cafés, restaurants, parks, cultural exhibitions).
  • Meetings in private residences, hotel rooms, private farmhouses, or secluded vehicles are strictly prohibited.

4. Booking Confirmation Policy

Submitting an online form does NOT guarantee an outing or create a binding contract. A booking is considered confirmed ONLY when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

  • The operator presents an available, interested companion.
  • Both parties explicitly agree on the activity, public venue, date, and start/end time.
  • Both parties agree on associated costs, ticket passes, and companion compensation.
  • Both parties acknowledge the SaathScene Code of Conduct.

5. Right to Decline and Terminate Outings

Both customer and companion maintain the absolute right to decline any proposal without penalty. During an outing, either participant may terminate the session immediately if they feel uncomfortable, disrespected, or if boundary guidelines are compromised.

6. Operational Status & Legal Disclaimer

SaathScene is an early-stage pilot validation project. These terms represent our foundational operating standards and are subject to continuous operational enhancement and legal counsel review.
