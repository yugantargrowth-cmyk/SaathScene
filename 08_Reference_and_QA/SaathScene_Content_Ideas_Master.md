# SaathScene --- Customer + Companion Content Ideas

## Master Content Strategy & Creative Direction

**Brand line:** Your next social scene starts here.\
**Core idea:** SaathScene helps people find company for real-life social
plans, while also inviting suitable people to become companions. It is
not only a Garba page and should not look like a collection of generic
graphic templates.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. The main problem to fix

Our content must speak to **two audiences**, not just one:

### Audience A --- Customers

People who want to go out, attend an event, explore a café, watch a
movie, or enjoy an activity but do not have the right company available.

What they need to understand: - What SaathScene is. - What kinds of
plans it can help with. - How the process works. - What it costs, once
pricing is confirmed. - How safety, boundaries, privacy, and companion
selection work. - How to make an enquiry.

### Audience B --- Potential Companions

People who enjoy meeting others, attending social events, exploring
places, and offering friendly, respectful company.

What they need to understand: - Who can apply to become a companion. -
What the role actually involves. - Whether it is paid, commission-based,
or otherwise compensated (publish only after confirmed). - What
availability, conduct, boundaries, and verification are required. - How
to apply and what happens next.

**Important:** Do not imply guaranteed matches, immediate availability,
completed background checks, established customer numbers, partnerships,
or confirmed earnings unless these are genuinely in place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. The visual direction: less template, more life

### Desired vibe

Warm, candid, modern, local, human, social, and a little cinematic. It
should feel like a real moment someone wants to be part of---not a
corporate presentation.

### Use more

-   Realistic photography and lifestyle images.
-   Friends laughing at a café, people walking around a market, a movie
    outing, board games, city evenings, event crowds, and Garba nights.
-   Natural expressions, movement, imperfect candid moments, warm
    lighting, and varied framing.
-   People in pairs or small groups, with enough context to understand
    the scene.
-   Gujarati/Indian cultural context when relevant: Navratri outfits,
    dandiya sticks, decorated venues, chaniya cholis and kediyus, food
    streets, cafés, and Ahmedabad city life.
-   A mix of close-up portraits, wide event scenes, over-the-shoulder
    phone moments, and candid action shots.
-   Genuine user-generated photos later, only with informed permission.

### Use less

-   Repeating the same teal rectangle, icons, and geometric layout on
    every post.
-   Posters made entirely from typography.
-   Stock-photo smiles that look staged or overly romantic.
-   Hearts, dating-app clichés, fake chat screenshots, fake reviews,
    fake testimonials, or fake customer counts.
-   Images that make the service look like a dating or escort service if
    that is not the service offered.

### Brand treatment

Keep the official SaathScene logo exactly as supplied in the Brand Kit.
Use the existing brand colours as accents, frames, small labels, or CTA
areas---not as a requirement to fill the whole image with blocks of
colour. Keep text short and readable. Let the photograph carry the
emotion.

### Photography / AI-image direction

Prefer natural, documentary-style images with authentic-looking Indian
adults (18+), believable clothing and settings, natural skin texture,
candid body language, realistic hands and faces, and no embedded text or
logos. Add approved brand text and the official logo afterwards in
layout/design software. Do not generate a replacement logo inside an
image.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Content pillars

Suggested starting mix across the first 20 posts: - **30% Customer
problem + relatable moments** - **20% Explain the service and how it
works** - **20% Companion recruitment** - **15% Experiences / local
lifestyle** - **10% Trust, safety, boundaries and FAQs** - **5% Direct
offer / enquiry CTA**

This is a starting hypothesis, not a proven formula. Review actual
enquiries and applications, then adjust.

# PART A --- CUSTOMER-ACQUISITION CONTENT

## A1. "The plan is ready. The company isn't."

**Format:** Photo-led carousel or single photo\
**Visual:** An adult dressed for an outing, checking their phone while
friends' plans fall through; natural, non-melodramatic.\
**Hook:** "Plan toh mast hai. Saath jaayein kiske saath?"\
**Message:** Wanting company for an outing is normal. SaathScene is
being built to help people explore social plans with suitable
companionship.\
**CTA:** "DM 'PLAN' and tell us what kind of outing you have in mind."\
**Purpose:** Relatability and customer enquiries.\
**Accuracy note:** Do not promise a companion is available until the
process can actually fulfil enquiries.

## A2. "You don't need to cancel the plan."

**Format:** Reel using 3--4 lifestyle shots\
**Visual:** Outfit ready → event/café on phone → hesitation → hopeful
city/event shot.\
**Hook:** "Kitni baar plan sirf company na hone ki wajah se cancel
kiya?"\
**Message:** Your interest in going out is valid, even if your usual
friends are busy.\
**CTA:** "DM 'PLAN' to ask how SaathScene works."\
**Purpose:** Awareness and inbound enquiries.

## A3. "What is SaathScene?"

**Format:** Photo-led 4-slide carousel\
**Slides:** 1. "Meet SaathScene." 2. "For plans you want to enjoy with
company." 3. "Cafés • movies • events • city outings • conversations."
4. "Tell us your plan. We'll explain the process and what's currently
possible." **Visual:** One strong lifestyle photo per slide; small brand
accent only.\
**CTA:** "DM 'INFO'."\
**Purpose:** Brand clarity.\
**Note:** Phrase as a service being offered only to the extent it is
operationally ready.

## A4. "Not every outing needs a whole group."

**Format:** Single photo\
**Visual:** Two adults chatting casually over coffee, clearly platonic
and relaxed.\
**Hook:** "Kabhi-kabhi bas ek achhi company kaafi hoti hai."\
**Message:** A café visit, a walk in a public place, or a social event
can be more enjoyable with company.\
**CTA:** "What kind of plan would you like company for?"\
**Purpose:** Communicate the value without making it sound romantic.

## A5. "Choose your scene"

**Format:** Photo carousel\
**Slides / scenes:** Café catch-up; Garba night; movie outing; food
street; bookshop/art/cultural event; city walk in a public place.\
**Hook:** "Tumhara scene kya hai?"\
**CTA:** "Comment your scene or DM 'PLAN'."\
**Purpose:** Help people picture how they might use the service.\
**Safety note:** Offer only activities the service is genuinely prepared
to support.

## A6. "Your friends are busy. Your plan doesn't have to disappear."

**Format:** Reel or single candid photo\
**Visual:** Phone calendar with a planned outing, then a lively
real-world scene. Avoid readable fake messages.\
**Hook:** "Sab busy hain? Samajh sakte hain."\
**Message:** SaathScene is exploring a way to make social plans easier
to pursue.\
**CTA:** "DM 'INFO' to understand the current process."\
**Purpose:** Address a common customer barrier.

## A7. "What happens after I DM?"

**Format:** Simple photo-backed carousel\
**Slides:** 1. "You send an enquiry." 2. "We ask about your plan,
preferences, date, and boundaries." 3. "We explain the options, price,
and availability we can genuinely offer." 4. "You decide whether to
proceed." **Visual:** Candid phone-use photo, close-ups, and clean text
overlays.\
**CTA:** "DM 'HOW'."\
**Purpose:** Reduce uncertainty and increase conversion.\
**Critical dependency:** Publish this only once the actual enquiry
workflow follows these steps.

## A8. "Before you book, ask us anything."

**Format:** Story Q&A sequence\
**Prompts:** "How does it work?" "What activities can I ask about?" "How
is pricing explained?" "What are the boundaries?" "How is privacy
handled?"\
**CTA:** "Send your question privately."\
**Purpose:** Identify objections and build a useful FAQ.\
**Rule:** Answer honestly; do not claim safety checks or protections
that are not implemented.

## A9. "A social outing, not a dating promise."

**Format:** Brand explainer post\
**Visual:** Two adults enjoying a casual public activity; no romantic
poses or hearts.\
**Hook:** "Company for a plan. No awkward assumptions."\
**Message:** State the service's non-sexual, respectful companionship
boundaries in clear language.\
**CTA:** "DM 'INFO' if this sounds like your kind of scene."\
**Purpose:** Set expectations and filter out mismatched enquiries.

## A10. "Ahmedabad, what would you go out for?"

**Format:** Story poll / question sticker\
**Options:** Garba; café; movie; food walk; art/cultural event;
something else.\
**CTA:** "Vote, then tell us what makes that plan difficult to
arrange."\
**Purpose:** Customer research and local relevance.\
**Do not:** Claim these options are all currently bookable.

## A11. "First, tell us what you actually want."

**Format:** Photo-led carousel\
**Message:** A good enquiry starts with the customer's plan, preferred
setting, timing, budget range (once defined), and boundaries.\
**CTA:** "DM 'PLAN'."\
**Purpose:** Encourage qualified enquiries instead of vague likes.

## A12. "The outing moodboard"

**Format:** 5-photo carousel, almost no graphics\
**Visuals:** Ahmedabad café, public Garba venue, movie entrance, food
street, cultural event. Use licensed/owned photos or properly generated
visuals.\
**Hook:** "Save this for the next time you want to step out."\
**CTA:** "Which scene should we feature next?"\
**Purpose:** Saves, shares, and lifestyle positioning.

# PART B --- COMPANION RECRUITMENT

## B1. "Love going out and meeting people?"

**Format:** Photo-led recruitment post\
**Visual:** Confident adult enjoying a public cultural event with
friends; natural, non-romantic.\
**Hook:** "Ahmedabad --- want to become a SaathScene companion?"\
**Message:** Invite adults who are friendly, respectful, reliable, and
comfortable accompanying people to agreed social activities.\
**CTA:** "DM 'COMPANION' to request the application details."\
**Purpose:** Generate companion applications.\
**Accuracy note:** Do not state pay, earnings, or guaranteed work until
terms are confirmed.

## B2. "Who makes a good companion?"

**Format:** Carousel with lifestyle photos\
**Qualities:** Respectful communication; punctuality; listening; comfort
in public social settings; respecting personal boundaries; reliability.\
**Hook:** "It's not about being the loudest person in the room."\
**CTA:** "Think this fits you? DM 'COMPANION'."\
**Purpose:** Attract suitable applicants rather than just maximum
applications.

## B3. "What does a companion actually do?"

**Format:** Reel or photo carousel\
**Possible duties:** Attend an agreed public social activity;
communicate clearly; respect boundaries; follow the agreed plan; contact
the service if an issue arises.\
**Hook:** "Companion banna exactly means kya?"\
**CTA:** "DM 'ROLE' for the role details."\
**Purpose:** Make the role concrete.\
**Dependency:** Finalize the role description, compensation,
cancellation policy, and conduct rules before publishing as definitive.

## B4. "This is not a dating or escort job."

**Format:** Clear FAQ post\
**Message:** Describe the service's actual non-sexual companionship
scope, conduct standards, and boundaries. Avoid shaming or provocative
wording.\
**CTA:** "Read the role details before applying."\
**Purpose:** Set expectations and reduce inappropriate applications.\
**Dependency:** Publish only if this matches the final written service
policy.

## B5. "A good companion respects boundaries."

**Format:** Photo-led post with short overlay\
**Message:** Consent, personal space, punctuality, respectful language,
privacy, and the right to stop an uncomfortable interaction matter.\
**CTA:** "Apply only if you are comfortable following these standards."\
**Purpose:** Values and trust.

## B6. "What happens after I apply?"

**Format:** 4-slide carousel\
**Suggested flow:** Application → human review → conversation/identity
and eligibility checks as actually implemented → acceptance and
onboarding if suitable.\
**CTA:** "DM 'COMPANION' to ask for the application link."\
**Critical rule:** Do not say "verified companion" or imply background
checks unless the checks are truly completed and documented.

## B7. "Companion FAQ"

**Format:** Stories with Q&A sticker\
**Questions:** Who can apply? What activities are involved? Is it paid?
What availability is expected? What are the safety rules? How is privacy
handled?\
**Purpose:** Learn what applicants need to know.\
**Dependency:** Publish confirmed answers on eligibility, pay, location,
scheduling, and selection.

## B8. "Your city, your interests, your social skills."

**Format:** Candid photo or Reel\
**Visual:** Adult at a café, event, art space, or public cultural
setting.\
**Message:** If you enjoy respectful conversation and social outings,
ask about the companion role.\
**CTA:** "DM 'COMPANION'."\
**Purpose:** Recruitment with lifestyle appeal.

## B9. "Reliability is attractive."

**Format:** Typography kept minimal over a candid image\
**Message:** Be on time, communicate honestly, respect the agreed plan,
and treat people well.\
**Purpose:** Set a professional tone for companion applicants.

## B10. "We're building SaathScene in Ahmedabad."

**Format:** Founder/build-in-public post\
**Visual:** Founder working on the service, scouting public venues,
preparing onboarding material, or planning the experience.\
**Message:** Share what is actually being built and invite customers and
potential companions to express interest.\
**CTA:** "DM 'PLAN' for customer interest or 'COMPANION' for role
information."\
**Rule:** Be transparent that the service is in an early stage if it is
not fully operational.

# PART D --- TRUST, SAFETY, AND FAQ CONTENT

## D1. "What SaathScene is---and isn't"

Explain the service clearly, including non-sexual boundaries and the
types of social outings it supports. Avoid misleading dating, escort,
therapy, or friendship-guarantee claims.

## D2. "Your preferences matter"

Explain how a customer can share activity preferences, comfort
boundaries, timing, and budget. Do not imply a match is guaranteed.

## D3. "Privacy is part of respect"

Describe exactly what personal information is collected, who can access
it, how it is used, and how a person can request help or deletion.
Publish only after a real privacy process exists.

## D4. "Plans, prices, and cancellations"

Publish transparent pricing, payment, cancellation, refunds, waiting,
and late-arrival rules after they have been decided. Never hide
important charges.

## D5. "Public meetups and safety"

Explain the real safety process: agreed public venue, clear start/end
times, emergency contact/escalation method, conduct rules, and incident
handling---only where implemented.

## D6. "A respectful experience has two sides"

Show that both customers and companions must respect boundaries,
communicate clearly, and behave appropriately.

## D7. "Questions people may be shy to ask"

Use anonymous question collection for practical concerns. Do not invent
answers or fake testimonials.

# 5. What each post should contain

For every content idea, the creator must provide: 1. Content ID and
intended audience: Customer / Companion / Both. 2. Business goal:
awareness / enquiry / application / trust / conversion. 3. One clear
hook. 4. A short caption in natural Hinglish; Gujarati where it
genuinely helps. 5. A single primary CTA and DM keyword. 6. Visual
format: photo-led post / carousel / Reel / Story. 7. Image brief with
scene, setting, people, emotion, framing, light, clothing, and cultural
context. 8. On-image text (short; ideally one hook, not paragraphs). 9.
Brand treatment: exact supplied logo file, restrained brand colours,
readable typography. 10. Status: idea / prompt ready / image generated /
reviewed / approved / published. 11. Any operational dependency or claim
that must be confirmed before publishing. 12. Metric to watch: profile
visits, saves, shares, customer DMs, qualified companion applications,
completed bookings---track only what actually happens.

# 6. Instructions for the creative master prompt

Use the content above as the strategy source. Do not just generate
another moodboard or a pile of similar geometric graphics.

**Required creative direction** - Build two explicit funnels: one for
customers and one for companions. - Keep Garba as a seasonal
sub-campaign only. - Prioritize lifestyle photography and realistic
candid moments; use graphic-only designs sparingly for explainers, FAQs,
and CTAs. - Create a varied feed: single photo posts, photo-led
carousels, Stories, and Reels. Avoid making every asset look like the
same Canva template. - Use authentic-looking Indian adults aged 18+ in
general promotional imagery, unless a specific adult eligibility policy
is approved. - Use the exact official SaathScene logo files from the
Brand Kit. Never redraw or approximate them. - Keep visual text minimal
and place it in the design afterwards; do not rely on AI-generated text
inside photos. - Do not invent customer reviews, results, partnerships,
safety checks, prices, earnings, or companion availability. - Create the
first 6 priority assets first: brand explainer, customer problem,
companion recruitment, how it works, companion role/FAQ, and one current
seasonal/local scene. - For each asset, provide the actual export where
tools support it, plus caption, CTA, image/video prompt, and production
status. - Track real file paths and real Canva URLs only. Mark
unavailable outputs as pending. - Keep the tone warm, human, modern,
local, inclusive, and non-judgmental. Avoid manipulative loneliness
messaging, dating clichés, sexualized visuals, or false promises. -
Measure success by qualified customer enquiries and companion
applications, not likes alone.

-   [ ] Confirm prices, payments, cancellations, refunds, and
    availability process.
-   [ ] Decide companion eligibility, compensation, application review,
    identity checks, conduct rules, and onboarding.
-   [ ] Establish privacy notice, data access, retention/deletion,
    emergency contact, and incident escalation process.
-   [ ] Decide whether service is strictly 18+ for launch.
-   [ ] Ensure Instagram DMs have a real owner and response process.
-   [ ] Ensure every published CTA goes somewhere that works.
-   [ ] Do not claim a person is verified unless the stated verification
    has genuinely occurred.
-   [ ] Review every design for spelling, cultural accuracy,
    readability, correct logo, and honest claims.
-   [ ] Check current event dates before publishing seasonal content.

## 8. Age policy: why start with 18+?

A social companionship service creates in-person interactions between
people who may not know each other. Launching with adults only makes
consent, identity/age checks, safeguarding, and liability simpler to
manage while the operating process is still new. It is a recommended
risk-control choice, not a claim that every service involving a minor is
automatically illegal.

If the business wants to serve under-18s, do not simply change the age
dropdown. First get appropriate legal/safeguarding advice and create a
separate process covering guardian consent, verified guardians, child
protection, data privacy, activity restrictions, supervision,
complaints, and rules preventing unsafe adult-minor interactions. A
safer initial policy is to serve adults 18+ only and make the age rule
clear in the form and marketing.
