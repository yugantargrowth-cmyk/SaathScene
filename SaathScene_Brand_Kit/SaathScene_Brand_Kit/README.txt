SaathScene Brand Kit
====================

Start here:
- 01_Logos/ — SVG vector masters plus PNG/JPG exports
- 02_Social/ — starter profile, post, WhatsApp share and cover assets
- 03_Guidelines/ — PDF and TXT brand notes

Palette:
Deep Teal #0F3D35 | Teal #14B8A6 | Sand #F4EDE7 | Slate #6B7280 | Charcoal #111827

SVG files are scalable vector artwork. PNGs are raster exports; icon PNGs are supplied at common sizes.
This is a starter concept kit. The S symbol is original concept artwork and should be reviewed/refined before launch.
Availability of trademarks, domains and social handles has not been checked.
