Privacy Notice

Last Updated: 11 October 2026 • Ahmedabad, Gujarat

Pilot Transparency Notice: This privacy document is a foundational starter statement for the SaathScene Ahmedabad pilot. We collect only what is strictly necessary to arrange safe public companionship outings.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a customer enquiry or companion application through our website, we collect minimal operational information:

  • Contact Information: Your preferred name, phone/WhatsApp number, and/or email address.
  • Outing Preferences: Desired social activity (e.g. Navratri Garba, café meeting), preferred date/time, and public neighborhood/venue in Ahmedabad.
  • Age Confirmation: Affirmation that you are aged 18 years or older.
  • Companion Profiles: Availability, social interests, and comfort preferences.

What We Never Collect Online: We never collect sensitive identity numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport), home addresses, financial credentials, credit cards, or passwords through our intake forms.

2. Why We Collect This Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Review your social companionship enquiry or application.
  • Evaluate mutual availability and comfort between customers and companions.
  • Contact you via WhatsApp, phone, or email to discuss details and agree upon terms.
  • Ensure all participants meet the minimum age requirement of 18+.

3. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not maintain a publicly accessible member directory or social wall. Your contact details are only shared directly with a prospective companion after both parties have explicitly agreed to an outing.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Enquiry data is retained only for the duration of the pilot coordination. You may request immediate correction or complete deletion of your submission at any time by contacting our operator.
