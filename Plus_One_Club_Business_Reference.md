## 1. Executive overview

**Plus One Club** is an early-stage, Ahmedabad-focused service that
helps adults aged **18+** find a companion for non-sexual social
activities. Examples include attending events, Garba, visiting a café,
or going to a public social outing with someone rather than going alone.

The service is intended to be welcoming, modern, local, and clear about
what it is---and what it is not. It is a social-companionship service,
not an escort or sexual service, and it should never imply romantic,
sexual, or guaranteed relationship outcomes.

-   **Brand:** Plus One Club
-   **Instagram:** `@plusoneclub.ahmedabad`
-   **Initial market:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India
-   **Audience:** Adults 18+ looking for a social plus-one, and adults
    interested in applying as companions
-   **Starting budget:** ₹0 unless separately approved
-   **Current stage:** Validate demand and build a small, trustworthy
    MVP before investing in a full platform
-   **Primary objective of the first experiment:** Learn whether real
    customers and suitable companions will enquire and whether safe,
    mutually agreed bookings can be arranged.

## 3. The problem and the proposed solution

### Customer problem to test

Some adults want to attend an event or go out but would prefer company.
Their friends may be unavailable, their plans may not align with others,
or they may simply want a companion for a particular activity.

### Proposed solution

Plus One Club provides a clear way to: 1. Submit an enquiry for a
specific social activity. 2. Explain the date, time, location
preference, duration, and expectations. 3. Review a potentially suitable
companion privately. 4. Agree on the activity, boundaries, price, and
logistics before confirming. 5. Meet in a suitable public setting and
follow the agreed plan.

**Important:** This is a proposed service flow. Do not claim that a
match, booking, background check, identity verification, or safety
guarantee exists unless that capability has actually been implemented
and verified.

## 4. Initial services and use cases

Potential use cases to validate include:

-   Attending a public event together.
-   Garba partner / festival companionship (a seasonal campaign under
    Plus One Club, not a separate brand).
-   Café visits and casual public outings.
-   Social or community events.
-   Other mutually agreed, non-sexual activities in public settings.

Each request should be reviewed individually. The business may decline
requests that fall outside its scope or cannot be arranged safely.

### Explicit service boundary

Plus One Club is for **non-sexual social companionship for adults 18+**.
Do not market or operate it as a sexual service, and do not suggest that
payment buys intimacy, romance, physical contact, or guaranteed
affection.

## 5. Customers and companions

### Customer audience

Adults aged 18+ in Ahmedabad who want a companion for a specific social
activity and value clear communication, boundaries, and transparent
arrangements.

### Companion audience

Adults aged 18+ who are interested in providing non-sexual social
companionship and can communicate clearly, follow agreed boundaries, and
participate responsibly.

### Matching principle

A match is a proposal---not a promise. Both parties must freely agree to
the activity and arrangements. Neither party should be pressured to
accept a match or continue an interaction.

## 6. Business model, pricing, and payment

The exact pricing and commission structure remain **to be validated**.
Do not invent a final rate card or claim market-tested prices before
collecting evidence.

Before any booking is confirmed, clearly communicate: - The activity and
purpose of the meeting. - Date, start time, duration, and public
venue/meeting point. - Who is participating and the agreed role of the
companion. - The total price, who pays whom, and any platform fee or
commission. - Cancellation, rescheduling, refund, and late-arrival
terms. - Relevant boundaries and a way to stop or report an
uncomfortable interaction.

### Booking and payment rules

-   An enquiry is not a booking.
-   A suggested match is not a booking.
-   A booking is confirmed only after both parties agree to the material
    details and the confirmation process is completed.
-   Do not charge for a mere suggested match unless a clearly disclosed,
    lawful, separately agreed fee is introduced later.
-   Never promise bookings, income, or successful matches.
-   Record the agreed terms so both parties can refer back to them.
-   Keep cancellation and refund terms visible before payment.

**Pricing status:** Open decision; test willingness to pay and the
economics of a real booking before locking prices.

## 7. Trust, safety, consent, and privacy

Safety is a core operating requirement, not just a marketing section.

### Before a meeting

-   Keep initial meetings in appropriate public places.
-   Encourage each participant to arrange their own transport.
-   Share the planned venue and expected end time with a trusted person.
-   Confirm activity, duration, boundaries, and price in writing.
-   Make it easy for either party to decline or cancel.
-   Do not publish personal contact details, private messages, or
    applicant information.

### During a meeting

-   Consent and boundaries apply throughout; either person can stop or
    leave.
-   No one owes physical contact, affection, private access, or
    additional time because money has been paid.
-   If someone feels unsafe, they should leave and contact appropriate
    local emergency or support services.
-   Do not pressure participants to move to a private location or change
    the agreed activity.

### Privacy and data minimisation

Collect only information needed to assess an application or arrange a
booking. Explain why each field is collected, who can access it, how it
will be used, and when it will be deleted.

The companion application may require an Instagram handle for **private
application review and contact only**. It must not be shown publicly or
shared with customers. An Instagram handle alone does not verify
identity. Restrict access to application data and establish a deletion
process.

Do not collect sensitive documents or unnecessary personal information
through a public spreadsheet or insecure custom form. Use access
controls and review the relevant legal obligations before scaling.

### Honest trust claims

Only say that an applicant is "verified," "background-checked," or
"safety-screened" if a defined process has actually been carried out and
its limits are explained. Never imply zero risk or guaranteed safety.

## 9. Website and MVP

### Goal

Build the smallest useful mobile-first website that explains the
service, captures appropriate enquiries, and supports companion
applications. Do not build a complex marketplace before validating
demand.

### Initial pages/sections

-   Homepage and clear explanation of the service.
-   How it works.
-   Customer enquiry call to action.
-   Companion application call to action.
-   Service boundaries and safety.
-   FAQs.
-   Privacy information and contact method.

### Initial workflow

1.  Visitor reads the service explanation and boundaries.
2.  Visitor chooses customer enquiry or companion application.
3.  A simple form collects only necessary information.
4.  Submissions go to a restricted-access tracking sheet.
5.  The operator reviews requests and follows up privately.
6.  If a suitable option exists, both sides agree on details.
7.  The booking is confirmed only after mutual agreement and the defined
    confirmation steps.

Do not show applicant information publicly. Do not promise automated
matching unless it is built and tested.

### Tools and build approach

-   **Antigravity CLI:** inspect the existing project, build and test
    the mobile-first website, and prepare deployment.
-   **Free hosting:** use a suitable free tier for the MVP, checking
    current limits before deployment.
-   **Google Forms:** customer enquiry and companion application.
-   **Google Sheets:** restricted-access operational tracker.
-   **GPT:** copy, planning, QA checklists, and troubleshooting.

Preserve existing work, never expose API keys or secrets, and do not
claim deployment succeeded until the live site has been checked.

## 10. The nine-day validation experiment

The nine-day plan is a short experiment to learn, not a guarantee of
profitability. Keep spending at ₹0 unless a cost is explicitly approved.

### Day 1 --- Validate the problem

Clarify the first customer use case and talk to potential customers and
prospective companions. Record real responses. Separate evidence from
assumptions.

### Day 2 --- Define the offer and rules

Write the service scope, draft pricing hypotheses, booking confirmation
flow, cancellation terms, FAQs, and safety boundaries. Mark untested
pricing as provisional.

### Day 3 --- Build website V1

Create a simple mobile-first landing page with working enquiry and
companion-application calls to action, clear service boundaries, and no
fabricated proof.

### Day 4 --- Set up forms and tracking

Create the customer enquiry form, companion application form, and
restricted-access tracker. Minimise data collection and document how
submissions will be used and deleted.

### Day 5 --- Prepare content strategy

Create content pillars, hooks, captions, carousels, Stories, and local
angles. Prioritise clarity and honest claims.

### Day 6 --- Produce the first content batch

Create and publish the strongest usable assets. Track actual outputs,
time spent, and production costs or AI credits.

### Day 7 --- Distribute locally

Reach relevant Ahmedabad audiences through appropriate community
channels and direct outreach. Follow platform rules and avoid spam or
unsolicited pressure.

### Day 8 --- Handle enquiries

Respond promptly, explain terms, assess suitability, and record the
reasons people proceed or drop off. Confirm a booking only when all
required details are mutually agreed.

### Day 9 --- Review and decide

Review actual data: relevant enquiries, suitable companion applications,
conversations, mutually agreed bookings, revenue collected, expenses,
safety concerns, and lessons learned.

Choose to continue, adjust, pause, or stop based on evidence---not views
alone.

## 11. Metrics and decision-making

Track: - Relevant customer enquiries. - Suitable companion
applications. - Enquiries that turn into substantive conversations. -
Proposed matches and mutually agreed bookings. - Confirmed bookings and
money actually collected. - Expenses and time spent. - Reasons for
rejection, cancellation, or drop-off. - Safety or privacy issues. -
Content published and the enquiries it generates.

Do not treat views, likes, or follower count as proof of business
viability. Do not invent conversion rates, demand, testimonials, or
revenue.

### Simple end-of-day review

Record: 1. What was completed? 2. What evidence was collected? 3. What
numbers changed? 4. What was the biggest lesson? 5. What should stop or
change? 6. What is the single highest-priority next action?
